Privacy policy

What we collect

When you request a demo or trial, through the form on this site or a LinkedIn ad form, we store what you enter: name, company, job title, phone, email, team size, city and what you need.

How we use it

Only to contact you about your request and arrange the demo or trial. We don't sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

Deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by replying to any message from us or through the site form, and we will delete it.
